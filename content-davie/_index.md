# Building mission-critical vessels for America's defense, security and maritime future through U.S. shipbuilding capacity and proven Arctic expertise.

## About

Davie Defense is a U.S. shipbuilder focused on strengthening America’s maritime industrial base. As part of Inocea Group, a British-owned marine industrial group with operations across the United States, Canada and Finland, Davie Defense combines global shipbuilding expertise with a long-term commitment to expanding U.S. shipbuilding capacity.

Through its modernization of Gulf Copper Shipyards in Texas, Davie Defense is expanding domestic shipbuilding capability, creating skilled manufacturing jobs, and strengthening the industrial base that supports U.S. maritime security.

## Milestones
### A New Era for American Shipbuilding
**June 1, 2026 — Galveston, Texas**


Davie Defense broke ground on the transformation of Gulf Copper Shipyards, marking the beginning of a new era for American shipbuilding, and one defined by investment, innovation, and a renewed commitment to U.S. maritime strength.


For more than 75 years, Gulf Copper has been a leader in ship repair, fabrication, and marine services. Davie Defense's planned $1 billion investment will transform the Texas shipyards into a world-class shipbuilding complex, creating the infrastructure needed to build the U.S. Coast Guard's Arctic Security Cutters (ASC) in the United States.


The Arctic Security Cutter is the U.S. Coast Guard's next generation heavy icebreaker, designed to operate in the world's most challenging maritime environments. The program will provide the U.S. Coast Guard with a modern icebreaker fleet to strengthen national security, enhance maritime safety, and ensure year-round access to the Arctic.

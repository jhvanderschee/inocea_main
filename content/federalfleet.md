# Enabling defense, government and humanitarian operations through the construction, operation and ownership of advanced mission critical ships.

## About

Federal Fleet is an international defense contractor operating a modern fleet of NATO-compliant combat support vessels, enabling strategic, long-range and expeditionary naval operations.

The company is part of Inocea, a privately held British marine industrial group with operations spanning the United States, Canada and Finland.

Federal Fleet owns and operates large combat support ships (CSS), integrated within allied naval command structures. Naval endurance depends on at-sea replenishment, logistics, medical support, aviation integration and command coordination at sea.

For decades, Federal Fleet's current fleet has met the support needs of allied navies around the world. The entire fleet is certified to the highest international standards and configured for sustained blue-water deployment.

Federal Fleet provides deployable combat support capability under long-term service agreements, expanding allied naval auxiliary capacity, while maintaining full NATO interoperability.

### Resolve class

![](/uploads/asterix.png)

Multi-role combat support ship Asterix

Asterix, delivered in 2018, is a NATO-compliant, multi-role combat support ship. Designed with high redundancy, it supports allied naval forces across multiple theaters - either with a fleet or independently. The vessel carries embarked personnel, integrates into allied command structures, and provides replenishment to a wide range of surface combatants.

#### Capabilities

- Replenishment at Sea (marine fuel, aviation fuel, ammunition, dry cargo)
- Concurrent multi-station, multi-liquid UNREP operations
- Task group aviation operations, maintenance and vertical replenishment
- Task group (NATO Role 2E) medical facilities
- Integrated command and control (C2) spaces
- Long-endurance blue-water deployment capability
- Force protection with enhanced multidomain surveillance and C-UAS systems

#### Operational Record

- 300,000+ nautical miles sailed
- 536+ replenishment and support evolutions
- 21 allied navies supported
- 11 named military operations
- 15 multinational exercises
- Zero days of unplanned downtime

## Capabilities

All vessels are classed by Lloyd’s Register (LR). Management systems are certified by LRQA under applicable ISO standards. Federal Fleet participates in the Green Marine voluntary environmental performance program.

{{< accordion >}}

### Replenishment at Sea (RAS)

All vessels are equipped for complex underway replenishment in open-ocean conditions. All replenishment systems meet ATP 16 / NATO interoperability standards.

- Marine fuel transfer
- Aviation fuel transfer
- Lube oil and freshwater transfer (Commodity replenishment)
- Ammunition replenishment
- Dry cargo and stores transfer
- Multi-station concurrent RAS operations, abeam and astern methods

### Aviation Operations

Aviation capability extends logistics range and operational flexibility. Each vessel supports rotary-wing operations including:

- Helicopter launch and recovery
- Vertical replenishment
- Aviation fuel handling and storage
- Integrated deck operations within naval task groups
- Task group aviation maintenance

### Medical Support

Medical capability enables the vessels to support both combat operations and multinational humanitarian missions. Capabilities include:

- Primary surgical and emergency response facilities
- Stabilization and treatment capability
- Support for sustained deployments
- Humanitarian and disaster relief medical response
- Task group medical and dental services

Each vessel possesses task group medical facilities capable of functioning as the primary medical support platform within a deployed task group.

### Command & Coordination

All vessels include integrated command and coordination spaces capable of hosting:

- Task group staff
- Operational planning elements
- Coalition liaison personnel
- Communications integration

These spaces support coordination of replenishment, aviation, and maritime security operations.

### Humanitarian Assistance & Disaster Relief (HADR)

The fleet is configured to support:

-  Emergency logistics delivery
-  Medical response operations
-  Evacuation assistance
-  Offshore coordination during natural disasters

Large cargo capacity, aviation integration and endurance allow sustained support in affected regions.

### Full Vessel Operations

Federal Fleet provides complete operational management of all vessels, including:

- Ownership and lifecycle asset management
- Technical management and engineering oversight
- Preventive maintenance programs
- Crewing and workforce training
- Marine assurance
- Regulatory and classification compliance

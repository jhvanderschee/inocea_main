# For over 75 years a leader in ship repair and marine services — Gulf Copper becomes the Texas builder of the U.S. Coast Guard's Arctic Security Cutters.

## Capabilities

From new construction to heavy repair, Gulf Copper covers the full life of a vessel
on the Texas Gulf Coast: drydocks, deepwater berths, fabrication shops and a
workforce that has kept commercial, government and offshore fleets at sea for
three quarters of a century.

### New Construction

![New Construction](/uploads/capabilites-01.png){:.parallax}

Purpose-built hulls and modules assembled under one roof, from first steel cut to
sea trials. The Arctic Security Cutter programme brings ice-class newbuild work to
the Gulf Coast, backed by the shipyard infrastructure and trades already in place.

---.wide

### Fabrication

![Fabrication](/uploads/capabilites-02b.png){:.parallax}

Structural steel, piping and outfitting packages built to drawing and delivered
ready to install. Plate shops, machine shops and blast-and-paint facilities work
side by side, so a component never has to leave the yard between stages.

---.wide

### Repair & Refurbishment

![Repair & Refurbishment](/uploads/refurb-01.jpg){:.parallax}

Drydocking, steel renewal, machinery overhaul and voyage repair for tugs, barges,
offshore units and government vessels. Round-the-clock crews keep unplanned
downtime short and scheduled dockings on their dates.

---.bigtext

Build in Texas for American Security. Where innovation, engineering, and shipbuilding excellence come together.

## Careers

The cutters will be built by people, not by programmes. Gulf Copper is hiring
across every trade and every shift, with paid training for craftsmen who want to
learn a second skill and a clear route from the shop floor into supervision.

### Welder

![Welder](/uploads/welder.png){:.parallax}

Structural and pipe welders for newbuild and repair work, in the shops and on the
docks. Certifications are tested and maintained in house, and welders rotate
across programmes rather than staying on one job.

---.wide

### Human Resources

![Human Resources](/uploads/human-resources.png){:.parallax}

Recruiters, coordinators and benefits staff who bring several hundred new
craftsmen through the gate and keep them there. The role reaches from first
interview to apprenticeship planning and long-service retention.

---.wide

### Fabricator

![Fabricator](/uploads/careers-04.png){:.parallax}

Fitters and fabricators who read the drawing, lay out the steel and hand the
welder a joint that closes. Plate, pipe and structural work across the full range
of the yard's programmes.

---.wide

### Painter

![Painter](/uploads/careers-03.jpg){:.parallax}

Blasters and coatings applicators working to marine specification, in the paint
halls and on the drydocks. Surface preparation, primer and topcoat systems for
hulls, tanks and deck structures.

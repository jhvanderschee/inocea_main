# Sata Shipbuilding delivers offshore wind foundations for the global transition to renewable energy and complex steel fabrication and industrial product outfitting for demanding Marine & Offshore environments.

## What we do

From Arctic-strength hulls to pioneering offshore energy solutions, including the world’s first production spar, the first floating foundation for wind turbine, and the first gravity‑based structures for ice-covered seas, we deliver complex, large-scale projects with unmatched accuracy, reliability, and pride.


*   ### Offshore solutions
    ![:background](/uploads/wwd-offshore.jpg)

    - World-class offshore structures from concept to delivery
    - Designed for performance in deepwater, Arctic and harsh conditions

*   ### Block Fabrication
    ![:background](/uploads/img-3934-square.jpg)

    - Precision cutting and panel lines.
    - Large block production for cruise ships, naval vessels, and offshore platforms.
    - Outfitting capabilities with a strong subcontractor network.

*   ### Shipbuilding & Launching
    ![:background](/uploads/wwd-shipbuilding.jpg)

    - Full shipbuilding capacity with launch rails, lifting towers, and quay access.
    - Experience in standalone builds and modular delivery.

*   ### Surface Treatment
    ![:background](/uploads/wwd-surface-01.jpg)

    - Blasting, priming, and painting halls for blocks and modules up to 30 × 30 m.
    - Climate-controlled storage ensuring long-term protection.

*   ### Engineering & Design Support
    ![:background](/uploads/wwd-design.jpg)

    - AVEVA Marine expertise, detail and concept design experience, hydrodynamic analyses.
    - Partnerships with leading Finnish design houses.

*   ### Certified Quality & Safety
    ![:background](/uploads/wwd-security.jpg)

    - ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, EN 1090, ISO 3834
    - DNV-audited processes, dimensional control with Leica instruments, and advanced welding technologies.

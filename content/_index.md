# Sata Shipbuilding delivers offshore wind foundations for the global transition to renewable energy and complex steel fabrication and industrial product outfitting for demanding Marine & Offshore environments.

## What we do

From Arctic-strength hulls to pioneering offshore energy solutions, including the world’s first production spar, the first floating foundation for wind turbine, and the first gravity‑based structures for ice-covered seas, we deliver complex, large-scale projects with unmatched accuracy, reliability, and pride.

## Our expertise spans across industries, delivering mission-critical steel solutions worldwide:

*   **Offshore Renewables**
    Floating spar platforms and wind turbine foundations.

*   **Oil & Gas Exploration & Production**
    Semi-submersibles rigs, jack-ups, spar hulls, and production units.

*   **Shipbuilding & Conversions**
    Hull blocks, modular sections, outfitting, ship conversion and complete vessel capability.

*   **Subsea & Offshore Systems**
    Subsea frames, carousels, jackets, suction piles, and complex structures.

*   **Port Infrastructure**
    Pontoons, floating platforms, barges and quay structures.

*   **Special Steel Projects**
    Direct leaching reactors, pressure vessels, process tanks, pressure vessels, tanks, autoclaves, reactors, debarking drums, conveyors, and heavy rotating equipment.

## SATA Shipbuilding has delivered projects for the world’s leading shipyards, energy majors, and industrial players:

*   **Offshore Energy**
    - 16 semi-submersibles, 5 jack-ups, and 3 drillships built
    - 14 Spars built and supported with 3 more
    - World’s first floating offshore wind foundation (Hywind, 2008).
    - Tahkoluoto Offshore Windfarm foundations, Finland.

*   **Shipbuilding, Ship Sections and Port Infrastructure**
    - Hull blocks for Finland’s shipbuilding industry (Cruise ships, RoPax ferries etc.)
    - Port infrastructure such as pontoons for European clients.

*   **Heavy Industry & Special Projects**
    - Large autoclaves, reactors, and pressure vessels for Metso Outotec and Neste.
    - Subsea frames, cable carousels, and jacket components for offshore energy.
    - Civil and industrial fabrication, including debarking drums, conveyors, and subsea structures.

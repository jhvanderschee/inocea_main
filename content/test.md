# Custom Built in the UK for Performance Without Compromise.

## About

Part of Inocea, one of the world’s leading shipbuilding groups, Enigma
Powercraft designs and builds bespoke, custom boats on Britain’s South
Coast, combining high-performance engineering with precision crafts-
manship.

Our team brings together some of the best talent from the worlds of
powerboat racing, luxury boatbuilding and military craft, combining
decades of specialist experience to turn ambitious ideas into exceptional
boats.

Built individually around their owner’s requirements, every Enigma is
engineered for private, commercial or competitive operation, delivering
an exceptional balance of speed, safety and adaptability.

## Dynamic stability, engineered for changing conditions.
Dynamic ballast systems have long been used in military, search-
and-rescue and other specialist vessels, where maintaining stability,
control and safety in demanding operating conditions is critical.


Enigma Powercraft is integrating this proven technology into its
high-performance craft, using actively managed ballast to optimise
vessel attitude and stability as conditions, speed and loading change.
The result is greater control, improved seakeeping and an additional
layer of safety without compromising performance.

## Suspension seating, engineered for the extreme.
We have developed our own proprietary suspension seating system
in partnership with Shark Suspension, engineered specifically for the
extreme loads encountered during sustained high-speed operation.
Combining advanced shock mitigation with exceptional ergonomics,
the system delivers maximum occupant safety and comfort without
compromising the performance of the craft.

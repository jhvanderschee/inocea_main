SH Minerva is an Arctic Expedition Cruise vessel delivered to Swan Hellenic in 2021 as part of a three-vessel series. The vessel’s high ice class (PC5) ensures safe operation in extreme climatic conditions, ranging from tropical waters to polar regions with heavy ice fields. SH Minerva meets the latest environmental requirements and is fully self-sufficient for up to 40 days or approximately 800 nautical miles. The vessel features 76 passenger cabins and has a maximum capacity of 280 persons. Alongside its technical capabilities, SH Minerva offers elegant interior design and high-quality accommodation tailored for expedition cruising. The sister ship SH Vega, identical in design, was delivered in 2022, followed by the third vessel of the series, SH Diana.

## Cruise vessel

- Delivery year: 2021
- Length: 113.6 m
- Breadth: 20.2 m
- Draught: 5.7 m
- Gross tonnage: 10,600
- Persons on board: max 280
- Ice class: PC5

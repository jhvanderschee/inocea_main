The icebreaker Polaris was delivered in 2016 to the Finnish Transport Agency and later transferred to Arctia Oy. IB Polaris is the world’s first icebreaker designed to operate on liquefied natural gas (LNG), representing a significant step forward in environmentally sustainable icebreaking technology. The vessel’s innovative hull form is designed to reduce ice resistance, while three Azipod® propulsors provide excellent maneuverability and precise control in demanding conditions. In addition to icebreaking, IB Polaris is also equipped for oil recovery and rescue operations.
 
## Icebreaker 

- Delivery year: 2016 
- Length: 110 m 
- Breadth: 24.4 m 
- Draught: 8 m 
- Gross tonnage: 9,300 
- Crew: 16 (+8) persons 
- Ice class: PC4

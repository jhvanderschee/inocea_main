Baltika is an icebreaking emergency and rescue vessel delivered to the Russian Ministry of Transport in 2014. The ship’s unconventional oblique hull form, combined with an asymmetrical hull and three azimuthing propulsors, enables the vessel to open significantly wider ice channels and maintain excellent maneuverability in challenging ice conditions. In addition, Baltika is equipped with advanced oil recovery, emergency and rescue capabilities, making it a highly versatile multipurpose vessel.
 
## Icebreaker 

- Delivery year: 2014 
- Length: 76.4 m 
- Breadth: 20.5 m 
- Draught: 6.3 m 
- Gross tonnage: 3,800 
- Crew: 24 (+12) persons 
- Ice class: Icebreaker 6

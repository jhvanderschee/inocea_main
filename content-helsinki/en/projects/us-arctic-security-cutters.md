The Arctic Security Cutter (ASC) program is a major U.S. Coast Guard initiative to expand and modernize its polar icebreaking fleet. Helsinki Shipyard plays a key role in the program, with two vessels constructed in Helsinki in close collaboration with Sata Shipbuilding. An additional three Arctic Security Cutters will be built in the United States at Inocea Group’s Gulf Copper shipyard in Texas.

Based on a proven icebreaker platform developed at Helsinki Shipyard, the ASC design reflects decades of Finnish expertise in operating in the world’s harshest environments.

The cutters will support critical missions including national security, maritime safety, and sustained access to Arctic regions.

The first vessel is scheduled for delivery in 2028, with the program continuing into the next decade as part of a broader fleet expansion effort. Through this program, Helsinki Shipyard contributes to strengthening transatlantic industrial cooperation while reinforcing its position as a leading builder of advanced Arctic vessels.

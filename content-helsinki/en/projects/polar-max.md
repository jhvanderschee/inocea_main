Polar Max is a next-generation heavy icebreaker currently under construction at Helsinki Shipyard. Designed as one of the most advanced and capable vessels in its class, the 138.5-meter multipurpose icebreaker will support year-round Arctic operations, including icebreaking, scientific research, environmental response, and maritime emergency services.

Polar Max demonstrates Helsinki Shipyard’s ability to deliver large and complex vessels on accelerated schedules while maintaining high-quality standards. The project represents a unique transatlantic collaboration, with the hull built at Helsinki Shipyard and final outfitting completed at Davie’s shipyard in Québec, combining Finnish expertise with Canadian industrial capability. Davie is an Inocea Group company.

The vessel is part of Canada’s National Shipbuilding Strategy and will be delivered to the Canadian Coast Guard by 2030, strengthening Arctic presence and operational readiness in demanding polar conditions. As one of the most significant icebreaker projects in recent decades, Polar Max highlights the yard’s leading role in Arctic shipbuilding.

SH Diana is the third vessel in the Swan Hellenic Expedition Cruise series, delivered in 2023. Slightly larger than her sister ships SH Minerva and SH Vega, the vessel features a PC6 hull, enabling worldwide travel in demanding and extreme weather conditions. The design emphasizes safe operations in sensitive environments while minimizing environmental impact. SH Diana offers premium amenities with elegant interiors and large outdoor spaces. Compared to her sisters, Diana has a capacity of 350 persons and 96 passenger cabins in total, ensuring passenger comfort on longer voyages.

## Cruise vessel 

- Delivery year: 2023 
- Length: 124.95 m 
- Breadth: 20.2 m 
- Draught: 5.7 m 
- Gross tonnage: 12,200 
- Persons on board: max 350
- Ice class: PC6

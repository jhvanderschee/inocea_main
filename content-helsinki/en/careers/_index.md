# Careers

The shipyard needs a wide range of expertise – professionals in technology, design, construction, and support functions.

---

## Built in Helsinki

Helsinki Shipyard is the global leader in designing and building specialized, ice-capable vessels, with around half of the world’s icebreakers in service built at our yard. We deliver tailor-made, high-quality ships engineered for the most extreme operating conditions.

Our work spans the full lifecycle of complex vessels – from advanced engineering to construction, outfitting, and commissioning – bringing together skilled professionals across disciplines. Working at Helsinki Shipyard means being part of a team where expertise, collaboration, and craftsmanship are central.

We are currently building a heavy Polar Max icebreaker for the Canadian Coast Guard, with construction underway in Helsinki and final completion in Canada. Construction of two Arctic Security Cutter-class icebreakers for the U.S. Coast Guard will begin in 2026.

Join our community of shipbuilders – world-class expertise in the heart of Helsinki since 1865.

## Why work with us?

1.  ### One team from design to build
    At Helsinki Shipyard, design and construction work side by side under one roof. You will collaborate closely with colleagues across disciplines and see the bigger picture.  
1.  ### Work in the heart of Helsinki
    Work at a historic shipyard in the heart of Helsinki. The shipyard is easily accessible by public transportation, bicycle, or on foot, with the city right outside the gate. 
1.  ### Witness the process
    Your work has a visible impact. From the first drawings to the final launch, you can see how your contribution helps bring a vessel to life.
1.  ### Work locally, collaborate globally
    As part of the Inocea Group, you’ll collaborate with colleagues, shipyards, and customers especially across Finland and North America. 
1.  ### Work at the forefront of specialized shipbuilding
    Work on highly specialized vessels for the most extreme operating conditions alongside experts in shipbuilding, engineering, and technology.

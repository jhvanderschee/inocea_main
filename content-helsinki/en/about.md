Global leader in icebreakers and specialized vessels

Helsinki Shipyard is the global leader in icebreakers and specialized vessels for the most demanding commercial and governmental customers.

Located in central Helsinki and backed by more than 160 years of maritime heritage, we continue to shape the future of specialized and Arctic shipbuilding. Our world-class design and engineering know-how, advanced systems integration, and disciplined project execution set us apart and enable us to deliver highly customized vessels on time and on budget. This includes cruise ships, yachts and naval vessels for both government and commercial customers.

---

## Inocea
### Inocea Group

We are part of Inocea, a privately owned international marine industrial group co-owned by Alex Vicefield, Chairman and CEO, and James Davies, President, CFO and founding partner. The group operates in Canada, the United States and Finland and is headquartered in the United Kingdom.

In Finland, Inocea’s operations cover Helsinki Shipyard in Helsinki and Sata Shipbuilding in Pori, which together form a single, integrated shipbuilding platform, focused on high-complexity, specialized vessels for demanding marine environments. Together, our operations in Helsinki and Pori enable true end-to-end delivery — from design and engineering to production, outfitting, and final handover.

## Leadership

### Team
Helsinki Shipyard and Sata Shipbuilding are managed by a joint leadership team. With deep experience in complex industrial programs, Arctic shipbuilding, and international operations, the team is responsible for translating Inocea’s long-term strategy into disciplined execution across Finland.

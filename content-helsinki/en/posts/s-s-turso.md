Today, Friday, September 6, the Turso tradition association came to thank the shipyard for its excellent support and for making the ship's 80-year docking possible.

Special thanks went to all those involved in the project for their professionalism and kindness. We also received the memories shown in the picture, for which we will find key placement locations at our shipyard.

SS Turso was floated out of the yard's dry dock on August 30.

Esko Karvonen has left his position as company CEO on 1.10.2021

Helsinki Shipyard is looking for a new CEO for the shipyard and CFO Simo Rastas is appointed for the transition period as acting CEO.

The shipyard is preparing for the impending handover of the first Swan Hellenic’s expedition cruise ship and the launching of the next ship in the series. Blocks of the third vessel in the series are being built by our subcontractor in Poland, from where they will be transported to Helsinki by water.

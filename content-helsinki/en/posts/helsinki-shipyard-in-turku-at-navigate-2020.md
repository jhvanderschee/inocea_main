Helsinki Shipyard will participate in Navigate 2020 event in Turku 22.-23.1.2020. You can find us at booth A/31.

At our booth you can meet people from our shipyard, who will be happy to tell you how we are doing and about the job opportunities at the shipyard. Also attending the event is _Simo Mattila_ who is one of the ambassadors for Finnish Marine Industries. You can ask Simo about his work at Helsinki Shipyard.

At this Navigate 2020 event you also have the possibility to hear the thoughts of our COO _Esko Karvonen_ who will be participating an interesting panel discussion with participants from Finland's leading shipyards. The panel discussion will take place on Wednesday 22.1.2020 at 14:00.

See you in Turku!

Helsinki Shipyard's operations are now crystallized in an entertaining animation, which you can check out at the link below.  
[Laivanrakennus – tie telakan portilta laivan luovutukseen](https://youtu.be/jtdoik6qI1w)  
The animation reflects the spirit of the Helsinki shipyard and we also hope that the most important thing will be conveyed to our viewers - we build ships responsibly together, not forgetting that it must be fun.  
Eero Heikkinen from Flake Oy, familiar from the look of Helsinki Shipyard, is responsible for the graphics and production of the animation.

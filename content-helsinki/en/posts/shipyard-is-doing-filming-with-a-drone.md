Helsinki Shipyard Oy has decided that it is time to make a new shipyard marketing video.

The matter was agreed with the Helsinki production company in October, and the filming of the video will begin in November 2022 and will be done by the end of March 2023.

The filming will be done by for example a drone, which will be flown over the shipyard and in the nearby terrain during the time mentioned above. So if you see a drone flying over the shipyard, there is a very high probability that it is related to this.

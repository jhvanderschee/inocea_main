Representatives of the United States Coast Guard (USCG) will arrive at Helsinki Shipyard on Friday, June 3, to conduct security inspections at NB517 - SH VEGA.

USCG inspections ensure that our new ship also complies with strict US safety regulations. Inspections will be conducted over the weekend 3-5 June.

In connection with USCG inspections, we are also testing the NB517 - SH Vega alarm sound. The alarm sound test is on Friday 3.6. from 19:00 to 21:00 and the sound will be heard in the vicinity of the yard. We apologize for the inconvenience.

The address of the shipyard is Laivakatu 1, 00150 Helsinki

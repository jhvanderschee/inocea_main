**SEATRADE CRUISE GLOBAL, POSTPONED DUE TO THE GLOBAL PANDEMIC, LAUNCHED ON TUESDAY, 27 SEPTEMBER IN MIAMI, FLORIDA.**

The number of participants in this big event in the cruise world has been affected by e.g. travel restrictions imposed on EU citizens following the pandemic. Due to this, not all representatives of Finnish companies have been able to travel from Finland to Miami, but fortunately some companies have the opportunity to use a local representative.

Helsinki Shipyard has its own stand at the fair, which we started preparing well before the postponement of the Setrade Cruise event. We have Ulla Lainio from Business Finland as our representative in our exhibition stand.

According to Ulla, the fair has started well, and the shipbuilding videos shown in our booth have attracted the interest of visitors.

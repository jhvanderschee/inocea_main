### Proven multi-mission capability – MPPS concept

![MPPS concept](/uploads/mpps.jpg){:.parallax}

Our Multi-Purpose Polar Ship (MPPS) concept is a versatile platform designed for naval and government missions in Arctic and other demanding environments. Combining icebreaking capability, operational flexibility, and mission adaptability, MPPS can support a wide range of tasks, from patrol and surveillance to logistics, research, training, and emergency response.

The MPPS-100 is a proven vessel concept with a strong operational track record in demanding northern environments. Based on an offshore supply vessel platform, it combines excellent icebreaking capability, good open-water seakeeping, and versatile mission support features.

Across four generations of MPPS 100 development, the core design philosophy has remained consistent: robust main dimensions, a highly efficient polar hull form, and a reliable propulsion concept optimized for ice and open water operations. At the same time, major development steps have been taken in ship safety and survivability, automation, machinery and engine configurations, mission flexibility, and icebreaking and propulsion performance.

The proven platform reduces technical and schedule risk while supporting efficient project execution and reliable delivery. Combined with established production processes and a cost-efficient construction approach, this helps customers achieve both operational performance and lifecycle value.

Designed for missions including:
* Maritime law enforcement
* Search and Rescue
* Emergency towing and escort
* Icebreaking and ice management
* Scientific and governmental operations
* Diving, cable work, firefighting, oil & chemical recovery
* Replenishment, submarine rescue, and mine-related operations

# Icebreakers

![Icebreakers](/uploads/polaris-1200x676.jpg){:.parallax}

Helsinki Shipyard is globally recognized as a leader in icebreaking technology

We have decades of experience delivering the world’s most advanced icebreakers, Arctic vessels, and mission-critical ships for operators – engineered for extreme conditions and long service life.

Our expertise spans the entire vessel development and construction process, from concept design and icebreaking performance optimization to efficient production and delivery. By combining deep Arctic know-how with modern engineering and flexible design approaches, we help customers develop vessels that meet operational and environmental requirements while maintaining dependable performance in the most demanding conditions.

Safety, reliability, and lifecycle efficiency are integrated into every project, ensuring vessels are built to deliver long-term value and confidence at sea.

Advanced passenger vessels from cruise ferries to superyachts

Helsinki Shipyard has capacity to design and build advanced passenger vessels ranging from expedition cruise ships and cruise ferries to superyachts.

Our heritage in cruise ship construction, Arctic operations, and complex vessel projects enables us to create passenger vessels that offer outstanding comfort, safety, and performance. Whether carrying guests to remote polar destinations, operating on commercial routes, or delivering a unique private yachting experience, our vessels are designed for long-term value and dependable operations.

By engaging early in concept development, we help customers shape vessel solutions that are technically sound, commercially viable, and optimized for their intended mission. Our efficient build strategy further enhances cost-effectiveness, helping customers lower overall project costs and delivering additional value for our customers.

---

### Cruise vessels and ferry solutions

We specialize in small and midsize cruise vessels, expedition ships, and cruise ferries where quality, innovation, and operational efficiency are key to success.

Our expertise in Arctic shipbuilding enables us to develop vessels capable of operating safely and efficiently in remote environments, while delivering exceptional passenger experiences. We combine advanced engineering with sustainable technologies, including hybrid powerplant, energy-efficient systems, and alternative fuel solutions.

From expedition cruising in polar regions to cruise ferry operations, every vessel is designed to meet the operator’s specific brand, route, and service requirements.

---

### Superyachts

Our boutique approach to yacht building combines world-class craftsmanship with the capabilities and discipline of a major shipyard.

We deliver customized superyachts tailored to each owner’s vision, supported by experienced engineering teams, proven management and construction processes, and trusted European suppliers. Owners benefit from integrated involvement throughout the build process, while our scrutinized design and building expertise ensures efficient execution and consistent quality.

The result is a yacht that combines elegance, durability, and technical excellence, designed for the highest expectations and built to the highest standards.
